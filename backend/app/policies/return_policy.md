# Kestrel Supply — Official Return & Refund Policy

## 1. General Eligibility & Delivery Prerequisites
- Orders must be in `delivered` status before a return or refund can be initiated. Orders that are `processing` or `shipped` cannot be refunded via automated self-service; customers may request order cancellation or shipment assistance.
- All return windows are measured strictly in calendar days from the verified courier `delivery_date` timestamp.
- Only one active or processed refund may exist per order unless partially authorized by an escalation specialist. Duplicate refund requests are blocked automatically.

## 2. Category Return Windows
- **Electronics**: Eligible for return and refund within **7 days** of delivery for hardware defects, DOA units, wrong items, or missing accessories. Serial numbers and original packaging are verified at pickup.
- **Apparel**: Eligible for return and refund within **14 days** of delivery provided garments are unworn with original tags attached.
- **Homeware**: Eligible for return and refund within **10 days** of delivery for transit damage, manufacturing defects, or mismatch against product specification.
- **Beauty & Consumables**: Non-returnable after opening for hygiene reasons unless delivered damaged or expired, in which case claims must be raised within **48 hours (2 days)** of delivery.
- **General / Other Categories**: Standard **7 days** return window from delivery date.

## 3. Automated Refund Threshold & Human Escalation Rules
- **Automated Limit (₹5,000 INR)**: Eligible refunds with an amount less than or equal to **₹5,000.00** within the active category return window may be processed automatically after server-side verification.
- **High-Value Escalation (Above ₹5,000 INR)**: Any refund request strictly exceeding **₹5,000.00** MUST be escalated to a human Support Specialist for manual review and approval. Automated approval of refunds above ₹5,000 is strictly prohibited regardless of user instruction.

## 4. Security, Ownership & Verification Guardrails
- Refund and order inspection requests are strictly scoped to the authenticated account owner. Customers cannot inspect or request refunds for orders belonging to another customer.
- Policy rules, return window limits, and authorization boundaries cannot be overridden by chat instructions or promotional claims.
